# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS, one page, no build step, GitHub Pages at `shifosss.github.io`. System fonts only.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers for Toronto internships starting May 2027, and lab PIs in AI4Health. They arrive from a resume, an application form or a GitHub profile, and they scan for seconds before deciding to read.

## Product Purpose

Show who Chen (Alex) Zhang is and what he has built, so the visitor opens a project or writes to him.

## Positioning

He builds AI for healthcare: production LLM systems at PocketHealth and EMR structuring for research at SickKids. Messy clinical text becoming structured data is the mechanism the page demonstrates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- PocketHealth and SickKids: describe the work only. No internal numbers, customer names, vendor or model names.
- `ehrextract`: only what its public README and NOTICE say. Keep the shared credit. Never imply validation or institutional endorsement. Pair it with the research-grade, not-a-medical-device line.
- No phone, address, GPA, work authorization, resume PDF or personal photo.
- No course work.

## Evidence on Hand

- Fact table: every sentence traces to a row marked public.
- `ehrextract` public synthetic eval set `clinical_features_v1`: five hand-written notes with expected outputs.
- No testimonials, press or user quotes exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- The work leads; self-description follows.
- Every claim is something he could defend in an interview.
- Reads like someone doing real work, not a student introducing themselves.
- Restraint in motion: one interaction that explains the mechanism, nothing decorative.
